Dear Sir/Madam,

We, Md Tahidul Islam and Maksuda Akter, undergraduate students of the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, CCN University of Science & Technology, are writing to inform the Department that we are carrying out an independent, pre-registered research study titled:

"Does iterative self-training degrade a small multilingual language model faster in Bangla than in English? A pre-registered model-collapse study with Gemma-3-270m."

The study is entirely computational. It fine-tunes a small open-weight language model (Google Gemma-3-270m, used under its published licence) on public text corpora (Bangla and English Wikipedia and other openly licensed web text) and measures how the model's outputs change over repeated rounds of training on its own generated text. No human participants, personal data, surveys, interviews or clinical material are involved, and no data is collected from any person. We therefore understand that no ethics review is required, and we are notifying the Department for the record.

The computing is rented from a commercial cloud provider and is paid for by us personally; the study has no external funding and we have no competing interests to declare. We intend to submit the results to a peer-reviewed venue with the University affiliation.
