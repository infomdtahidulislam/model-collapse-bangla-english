What changed since round 2 (your round-2 review; REQ-012):
  • Country (P17) probes now use only settlements and administrative areas (towns, cities, districts, provinces). Languages, festivals, topics, transport lines, roads, stations, ships, rivers and mountains are gone, and so are overseas territories such as Bermuda and places with more than one country. Wrong options are present-day countries only (no Mandatory Palestine, no Inca Empire), and never the country of another place with the same name (there is also a Toronto in England).
  • Birthplace (P19): a wrong option can no longer contain, or lie inside, the true birthplace (no “Banff” when Wikidata only says “Canada”).
  • Occupation (P106): a wrong option can no longer be a broader or narrower job than a true one (“actor” for a “film actor”), or a kind of a job the person already falls under (“postdoctoral researcher” for a biochemist, who is a researcher). Wikidata can still miss a job (it lists Narendra Deva only as a writer), so a wrong option can still be true now and then: reject it as before.
  • Capital (P36): only places with a single capital in Wikidata. Historical states that list several capitals are gone (“The capital of the Portuguese Empire is Rio de Janeiro.”).
  • English “the”: place names that start with a number take it (“the 2nd arrondissement of Lyon”), and so do the places in country probes (“The Northwest Territories is located in …”).
  • Everything else is exactly as you read it in round 2, and your 10 rejected probes are replaced. Every change is listed in data/probes/repair_round2.json.
